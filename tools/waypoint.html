Before surface travel: hold stationary for 5 seconds of accepted GPS/DVL/VN measurements; brief interruptions pause progress. This checks stability, not surveyed accuracy.

Raw GPS unavailable

Cube pose comes from its flight controller. Cube local coordinates stay fixed. Jetson projects Cube position and VN heading onto one map with x=north and y=east using the GPS/DVL transform. Heading difference checks agreement, not independent accuracy. Pressure depth comes from the supervisor; Cube Down uses the Cube origin. EKF metrics are reported consistency values, not position error in metres. GPS separation alone cannot establish which estimate is correct.
