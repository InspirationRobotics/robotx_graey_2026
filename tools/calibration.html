Calibrate, align north, then prepare

VN-100 supplies external attitude. DVL supplies velocity, GPS anchors surface position and north alignment, and pressure supplies depth. Cube EKF combines bridge odometry with its onboard inertial measurements and controls the vehicle. A Cube heading comparison checks fusion consistency; it is not an independent compass reference.

Connecting…

1 · Calibrate VN magnetic sensor

  1. Keep the assembled sub powered, disarmed, kill engaged and ESC power off. Keep VN mounted in its normal position.
  2. Choose a place away from nearby metal and changing magnetic sources. Support the sub safely with room and tether slack for rotation.
  3. Click Calibrate VN. Wait for “Rotate now” before moving it.
  4. Over 120 seconds, slowly turn through yaw, pitch and roll: upright, inverted, left side, right side, nose up and nose down. Keep kill engaged throughout.
  5. Check that all six face counters reach at least five. Coverage collected does not certify accuracy. Settings remain volatile; this does not save flash.

Six-face coverage unavailable

The normal VN reader performs calibration itself. No competing serial reader or duplicate navigation node is launched. Attitude input and odometry are withheld during calibration.

2 · Prepare navigation and align north

  1. Place Graey floating at the water surface, stationary, disarmed and killed with ESC power off. Wait several seconds. Never confirm this on land or underwater.
  2. Click Prepare Navigation. This invalidates previous preparation, calibrates surface pressure and clears the prior session reference.
  3. Wait for qualified GPS, valid DVL bottom lock and fresh VN data. The page will explain missing inputs.
  4. When it asks for movement, deliberately use normal pilot controls to release kill and drive in MANUAL at the surface. Move steadily at ≥0.5 m/s, level and without turning. Allow ample clearance to stop.
  5. North alignment needs ≥5 consistent seconds and ≥2.5 m of accepted travel. Sideways motion is supported; GPS travel direction is not assumed to be the nose heading.
  6. Once aligned, stop, disarm and engage kill again. Preparation checks Cube position, depth, heading consistency and health before granting session waypoint permission.

Prepare never arms, restores ESC power or starts a mission. A new bridge session needs a new north alignment. A timed-out or cancelled preparation does not enable waypoint execution.

Cancel stops magnetic learning if calibration is active and withholds waypoint permission. During manual surface movement, Cancel does not stop thrusters or change pilot mode; use your normal controls and physical kill.

Before Start mission

  1. Verify actual heading, forward/right/down sensor axes and Cube position/depth against physical observations. Automatic north alignment assumes correct sensor mounting.
  2. Verify GPS rejection underwater and stable DVL/VN continuation in a short disarmed submerge/surface check.
  3. Open Waypoint mission, select a clear geographic target, review depth and distance, and confirm manual takeover/Abort is available.
  4. Only then deliberately release kill and grant ESC permission using normal controls. Start mission can request GUIDED and arming. Supervise the first short dive–transit–surface test with logs.

Physical calibration and wet commissioning remain required. GUI readiness is not proof of surveyed position or immunity to magnetic interference.
