1 · Calibrate VN → 2 · Surface GPS track alignment → 3 · Check navigation health

VN-100 supplies external attitude. DVL supplies velocity, GPS anchors surface position and north alignment, and pressure supplies depth. A straight surface GPS/DVL track establishes the north offset; afterward VN supplies orientation through bridge odometry. Cube EKF combines that odometry with its onboard inertial measurements and controls the vehicle. A Cube heading comparison checks fusion consistency; it is not an independent compass reference.

Connecting…

1 · Calibrate VN magnetic sensor

  1. Keep the assembled sub powered, disarmed, kill engaged and ESC power off. Keep VN mounted in its normal position.
  2. Choose a place away from nearby metal and changing magnetic sources. Support the sub safely with room and tether slack for rotation.
  3. Click Calibrate VN. Wait for “Rotate now” before moving it.
  4. Over 120 seconds, slowly turn through yaw, pitch and roll: upright, inverted, left side, right side, nose up and nose down. Keep kill engaged throughout.
  5. Check that all six face counters reach at least five. Coverage collected does not certify accuracy. Settings remain volatile; this does not save flash.

Six-face coverage unavailable

The normal VN reader performs calibration itself. No competing serial reader or duplicate navigation node is launched. Attitude input and odometry are withheld during calibration.

2 · Prepare surface sensors and run 10 m alignment

3 · Prepare navigation · floating surface only

  1. Float stationary, killed/disarmed with ESC off. Click Prepare Navigation to establish pressure zero and check sensors. This does not move Graey.
  2. Wait for qualified GPS, DVL bottom lock and fresh VN. Choose a straight 10 m corridor with at least 2 m additional stopping clearance. No obstacle avoidance is provided.
  3. Enable propulsion and arm in MANUAL using normal controls. Point the nose down the corridor and keep the joystick neutral.
  4. Click Run 10 m north alignment. Graey stays in MANUAL; the script controls forward/lateral/yaw inputs, holds relative VN heading and targets 0.4 m/s. Vertical input stays neutral.
  5. The bridge compares GPS coordinates with time-matched relative DVL displacement, accounting for sideways drift. It rejects inconsistent tracks instead of guessing north.
  6. Near 10 m the script brakes and checks a three-second stationary interval. Only a completed stop accepts the offset. Disarm and engage kill afterward; Prepare then checks Cube fusion and enables waypoint permission.

Kill, mode takeover and Cancel remain effective. GPS/DVL loss pauses motion and aborts after five seconds; the entire operation is limited to 60 seconds. Currents and GPS uncertainty can prevent acceptance. A failed run does not automatically retry or extend travel.

Prepare never arms, restores ESC power or starts a mission. A new bridge session needs a new north alignment. A timed-out or cancelled preparation does not enable waypoint execution.

3 · Navigation health

Waiting for supervisor status.

After alignment completes, disarm and engage kill. The running preparation job verifies fusion automatically; do not recalibrate VN or reset Prepare between these steps.

Recover DVL position · keep north alignment

After a DVL continuity fault, float at the surface, disarm, engage kill and keep ESC power off. Hold still with good bottom lock for at least two seconds. Check the floating-surface confirmation above, then click Recover DVL position.

Keeps this session's VN calibration, north alignment and pressure baseline. Discards uncertain position and waits for newly qualified GPS to re-anchor it, then checks Cube fusion before enabling a new mission. No rotation or new heading entry is needed. If VN continuity or the north reference was lost, use Prepare and align north again. Recovery cannot run during a mission.

Before Start mission

  1. Verify actual heading, forward/right/down sensor axes and Cube position/depth against physical observations. Automatic north alignment assumes correct sensor mounting.
  2. Verify GPS rejection underwater and stable DVL/VN continuation in a short disarmed submerge/surface check.
  3. Open Waypoint mission, select a clear geographic target, review surface clearance and distance, and confirm manual takeover/Abort is available.
  4. Only then deliberately release kill and grant ESC permission using normal controls. Start mission can request GUIDED and arming. Supervise a short surface-only hold and waypoint test with logs; stop on any descent.

Physical calibration and wet commissioning remain required. GUI readiness is not proof of surveyed position or immunity to magnetic interference.
