1 · Calibrate VN → 2 · Learn Cube north → 3 · Check navigation health

VN-100 supplies external attitude. DVL supplies velocity, GPS anchors surface position and north alignment, and pressure supplies depth. Cube establishes a compass-based north reference during learning; afterward VN supplies orientation through bridge odometry. Cube EKF combines that odometry with its onboard inertial measurements and controls the vehicle. A Cube heading comparison checks fusion consistency; it is not an independent compass reference.

Connecting…

1 · Calibrate VN magnetic sensor

  1. Keep the assembled sub powered, disarmed, kill engaged and ESC power off. Keep VN mounted in its normal position.
  2. Choose a place away from nearby metal and changing magnetic sources. Support the sub safely with room and tether slack for rotation.
  3. Click Calibrate VN. Wait for “Rotate now” before moving it.
  4. Over 120 seconds, slowly turn through yaw, pitch and roll: upright, inverted, left side, right side, nose up and nose down. Keep kill engaged throughout.
  5. Check that all six face counters reach at least five. Coverage collected does not certify accuracy. Settings remain volatile; this does not save flash.

Six-face coverage unavailable

The normal VN reader performs calibration itself. No competing serial reader or duplicate navigation node is launched. Attitude input and odometry are withheld during calibration.

Legacy heading-reference controls

While killed, disarmed and ESC-off, point the level nose along an independently known true bearing. Enter it below and click Capture north. GPS, pressure and DVL bottom lock are not required for this capture. A stationary single GPS cannot supply this bearing. Keep VN powered continuously while carrying Graey to the water; a restart, calibration or VN data interruption invalidates the capture.

For land Capture north, keep Graey level, stationary, killed and disarmed with ESC off. DVL and pressure are not needed. For legacy surface capture during Prepare, bottom lock and surface depth are required. Align its nose with a known true-north bearing. Measure away from vehicle electronics; use true north, not magnetic north or GPS course while stationary.

Click Capture north below. It records the known bearing after a stationary VN check, without starting odometry or preparing water depth. Then carry Graey to water with VN continuously powered and use Prepare. If you choose the legacy surface method instead, Prepare captures this bearing at the water surface.

3 · Prepare navigation · floating surface only

For known true heading, follow the stationary instructions above. GPS/DVL alignment uses the automatic maneuver below.

  1. Float stationary at the water surface, disarmed and killed with ESC power off. Click Prepare Navigation to establish pressure depth.
  2. Wait for qualified GPS, DVL bottom lock and fresh VN data.
  3. Choose a clear surface corridor and enter its travel limit below, leaving additional stopping clearance. The script has no obstacle avoidance. A small pool may not provide enough room; never increase the limit to force alignment.
  4. Deliberately enable propulsion and arm in MANUAL using normal controls. Keep the joystick neutral and supervise physical kill and Abort.
  5. Click Run automatic north alignment. The script holds relative VN heading and controls forward speed toward 0.65 m/s, for at most 20 seconds of movement. It stops on accepted alignment, excessive rotation, speed or travel limits, or sensor/control loss.
  6. The script uses bounded DVL velocity braking and checks stationary DVL/GPS for three seconds. This is not geographic position hold. After completion, disarm and engage kill; preparation verifies Cube fusion before granting waypoint permission.

Prepare never arms, restores ESC power or starts a mission. A new bridge session needs a new north alignment. A timed-out or cancelled preparation does not enable waypoint execution.

2 · Compass Learn · Cube north to VN

  1. First identify and verify the selected Cube-connected compass, its mounting and magnetic interference. Compass acquisition and the primary compass must already be enabled; the button will not enable an unidentified compass or reboot Cube.
  2. Disarm, engage kill, ESC off for initial setup. Start temporarily selects compass yaw and sets COMPASS_LEARN=3. Values 1 and 2 for COMPASS_LEARN are not used. Navigation output and waypoint permission remain suspended.
  3. Enable propulsion normally and drive at the surface in armed MANUAL, with turns and good GPS reception. You do not need to stay disarmed for learning or north capture. Do not use a waypoint or position-hold mode while learning.
  4. After the actual firmware CompassLearn: Finished message and learn readback 0, the bridge automatically captures north from consistent paired Cube/VN readings. No button or disarm is required for this passive capture. If readings disagree or are stale, capture waits rather than accepting a bad reference.
  5. Once north is captured, stop, disarm, engage kill and switch ESC power off. The bridge automatically restores ExternalNav yaw and verifies the readbacks; it never disarms or changes sources while you drive. Then use step 3. If learning fails, use Cancel under kill/disarm/ESC-off.

Setup uses live safety telemetry; no confirmation checkbox is required. Manual driving and automatic north capture happen after setup.

Waiting for bridge compass-learning status.

3 · Prepare and check navigation health

Float at the actual surface, killed and disarmed with ESC off and DVL bottom lock. This step establishes pressure zero and checks Cube fusion. It keeps the Cube-derived VN north reference; it does not repeat magnetic calibration or ask for a manual heading.

Waiting for supervisor and bridge status.

The supervisor supplies status and continues checking health during the mission. Readiness does not enable ESC power or start a mission.

Recover DVL position · keep north alignment

After a DVL continuity fault, float at the surface, disarm, engage kill and keep ESC power off. Hold still with good bottom lock for at least two seconds. Check the floating-surface confirmation above, then click Recover DVL position.

Keeps this session's VN calibration, north alignment and pressure baseline. Discards uncertain position and waits for newly qualified GPS to re-anchor it, then checks Cube fusion before enabling a new mission. No rotation or new heading entry is needed. If VN continuity or the north reference was lost, use Prepare and align north again. Recovery cannot run during a mission.

Before Start mission

  1. Verify actual heading, forward/right/down sensor axes and Cube position/depth against physical observations. Automatic north alignment assumes correct sensor mounting.
  2. Verify GPS rejection underwater and stable DVL/VN continuation in a short disarmed submerge/surface check.
  3. Open Waypoint mission, select a clear geographic target, review depth and distance, and confirm manual takeover/Abort is available.
  4. Only then deliberately release kill and grant ESC permission using normal controls. Start mission can request GUIDED and arming. Supervise a short surface-only hold and waypoint test with logs; stop on any descent.

Physical calibration and wet commissioning remain required. GUI readiness is not proof of surveyed position or immunity to magnetic interference.
